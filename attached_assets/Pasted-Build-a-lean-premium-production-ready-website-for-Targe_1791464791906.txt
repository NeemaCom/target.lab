Build a lean, premium, production-ready website for Target Lab, an AI-native technology and innovation company.

BRAND

TARGET LAB

Building the Next Generation Solutions.

Target Lab builds AI-native digital solutions, software and technology ventures for businesses, institutions and emerging markets.

Target Lab is both:

A builder of digital and AI-native solutions for organisations
A technology venture builder developing its own products

Do not position Target Lab as a generic IT consultancy or software outsourcing company.

The brand should feel like a small, highly capable technology studio that can take a problem and build the solution.

WEBSITE OBJECTIVE

This is intentionally a lean website.

Do not overbuild it.

The website's primary purposes are:

Explain what Target Lab does.
Establish credibility.
Show the kind of problems we build solutions for.
Showcase Cush as an existing venture.
Give prospective clients and partners a simple way to contact us.

The site should be concise, visually strong and easy to navigate.

Do NOT create:

Large corporate dashboards
Complex tables
Pricing tables
Extensive service directories
Multiple layers of navigation
Blog infrastructure
Complex CMS
Customer portals
Large case-study databases
Excessive forms

Build only what is necessary.

SITE STRUCTURE

Use a simple single-page website with optional lightweight pages if genuinely necessary.

Primary navigation:

Home | What We Build | Ventures | About | Contact

The homepage should contain the majority of the experience.

HERO

Large, confident hero.

TARGET LAB
Building the Next Generation Solutions.

Supporting copy:

Target Lab is an AI-native technology company building intelligent digital solutions and technology ventures for the next generation.

Buttons:

Build With Us

Explore Cush

The hero should have a subtle, sophisticated digital visual.

Do not use robots, brains, circuit boards or generic AI imagery.

WHAT WE BUILD

Keep this section simple.

Headline:

We build what comes next.

Short introduction:

From AI-native applications to intelligent business systems, we turn important problems into practical digital solutions.

Use four simple capability blocks:

AI-Native Development

AI applications, agents, intelligent automation and AI-powered business systems.

Digital Products

Web applications, platforms, APIs and software products.

Data & Intelligence

Data systems, analytics, business intelligence and decision-support tools.

Technology Ventures

We identify important problems and build technology companies and products around them.

Do not turn these into huge service catalogues.

VENTURE

Create a visually distinctive featured venture section.

Built by Target Lab
CUSH

Cross-border financial identity infrastructure for economically invisible migrants.

Short description:

Cush is building infrastructure that helps people whose economic lives cross borders establish a more portable financial and economic identity.

Button:

Explore Cush

Cush should be presented as a real Target Lab venture and an example of the type of technology Target Lab builds.

Design this section differently from the services section so it feels like a portfolio/venture showcase.

Leave room for additional ventures in the future, but do not create empty placeholder cards.

APPROACH

Keep this very short.

Headline:

Start with the problem.

Copy:

We don't build technology because it is new. We build it because a problem is worth solving.

Then show a simple progression:

Understand → Design → Build → Launch

No elaborate process diagrams.

ABOUT

A concise section.

Headline:

A technology company built to build.

Copy:

Target Lab combines artificial intelligence, software engineering, product thinking and data to create solutions for organisations and markets changing rapidly around us.

We work with organisations that have a problem worth solving and build technology around the opportunity.

Keep this to two or three short paragraphs.

CONTACT

Very simple.

Headline:

Have something worth building?

Copy:

Tell us what you're trying to solve. Let's explore what we can build together.

Primary CTA:

Start a Conversation

Use either:

A simple contact form
Email link
Or both

If using a form, keep it minimal:

Name
Email
Organisation
What are you trying to build?

Do not ask for unnecessary information.

FOOTER

Minimal footer.

TARGET LAB

Building the Next Generation Solutions.

Links:

What We Build
Ventures

Contact

Include relevant social/company links only where they actually exist.

DESIGN DIRECTION

Target Lab should look premium, technical and understated.

Think:

Technology laboratory
Product studio
Engineering company
Venture builder

Not:

Traditional IT company
Marketing agency
Generic SaaS startup
AI hype website

Use:

Large typography
Strong visual hierarchy
Generous whitespace
Minimal navigation
Subtle motion
Clean grids
Abstract technology-inspired graphics
High-quality typography
Refined interactions

The website should feel confident without trying too hard.

Avoid excessive gradients and visual effects.

Avoid generic stock photography.

Avoid AI clichés.

COLOUR & TYPOGRAPHY

Create a distinctive but restrained visual identity.

Use a primarily neutral palette with one carefully selected accent colour.

Typography should be modern, highly legible and editorial.

The word TARGET LAB should have a strong typographic presence.

The tagline:

Building the Next Generation Solutions.

should be one of the most visually important elements on the site.

RESPONSIVENESS

The site must work exceptionally well on:

Desktop
Tablet
Mobile

Mobile should not simply be a compressed desktop version.

Typography, spacing and navigation should be intentionally designed for small screens.

TECHNOLOGY

Use:

Next.js
TypeScript
Tailwind CSS
React
Vercel

Keep the codebase lean.

Do not install libraries unless they provide a genuine benefit.

Use reusable components where appropriate, but don't over-engineer the application.

GITHUB

The GitHub repository is the source of truth.

Use clean, maintainable code.

Before modifying the project:

Inspect the repository.
Understand the existing structure.
Preserve working functionality.
Make changes incrementally.

After changes:

Run the build.
Run lint/type checks where available.
Fix errors before considering the work complete.

Use clear Git commits.

VERCEL

The website will be deployed through Vercel from GitHub.

Ensure the project can deploy cleanly through Vercel.

Do not rely on local-only functionality.

Keep environment variables out of the repository.

PERFORMANCE

Prioritise:

Fast loading
Minimal JavaScript
Optimised images
Good accessibility
Good Core Web Vitals
SEO fundamentals

Implement only essential metadata:

Title
Description
Open Graph image
Favicon
Robots
Sitemap

Do not build an elaborate SEO system.

SEO

Target Lab should naturally communicate relevance around:

AI-native development
AI solutions
software development
digital products
AI agents
business automation
technology ventures
digital innovation

Do not keyword stuff.

CONTENT PRINCIPLES

Write like a serious technology company.

Use short, confident sentences.

Avoid:

"leveraging cutting-edge technology"

"revolutionising the digital landscape"

"unlocking unprecedented value"

"transformative synergies"

"world-class solutions"

Use direct language.

Examples:

We build intelligent systems for problems that matter.

We start with the problem, not the technology.

We don't just advise on what's next. We build it.

IMPORTANT

Do not invent:

Clients
Partnerships
Awards
Statistics
Testimonials
Revenue
Certifications
Team members
Offices

Do not make Target Lab appear larger than it currently is.

The brand should communicate capability rather than size.

The final site should feel like a company that is small enough to move quickly and capable enough to build serious technology.

FINAL IMPRESSION

When someone visits Target Lab, within 10 seconds they should understand:

Target Lab builds next-generation digital and AI solutions.

Within 30 seconds they should understand:

Target Lab also creates its own technology ventures.

And after exploring the site they should think:

"I have a problem. These are the people I should talk to."

Build the website around that outcome.
