---
name: Target Lab brand direction
description: Product positioning and content guardrails from the Target Lab website brief.
---

Target Lab is a small, capable AI-native technology company that builds digital solutions for organisations and develops its own technology ventures. Do not position it as a generic IT consultancy or software outsourcing company, or imply a larger scale than stated. Keep the website concise, direct, and focused on problems worth solving. Do not invent clients, partnerships, awards, statistics, testimonials, revenue, certifications, team members, offices, or social links.

**Why:** These are explicit brand and content constraints in the user's brief and are not recoverable from application code.

**How to apply:** Use this positioning and these guardrails for future Target Lab website copy and visual decisions.
